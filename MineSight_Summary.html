MineSight — Project Summary

MineSight is an AI-based Smart Governance & Compliance Monitoring System for Coal Mines, designed specifically for the Software Track of the Smart India Hackathon 2026. It replaces the fragmented, paper-based governance systems in Indian coal mining operations with a unified, real-time digital platform.

1. The Problem

Mining operations involve complex parallel tasks: mine management, independent contractors, contract labour, safety inspections, and environmental monitoring. Currently, statutory compliance, safety observations, and worker attendance are handled via paper registers and spreadsheets. This leads to:

  • Data Inconsistency: The same fact exists in multiple places.
  • Delayed Decision-Making: Supervisors learn about problems days after they happen.
  • Compliance Gaps: Expired licenses go unnoticed until an audit or incident occurs.
  • Weak Field Monitoring: Verbal warnings are untracked and often ignored.

2. The Solution: A 2-Role MVP

To ensure a robust and realistic prototype, MineSight focuses on a highly focused 2-Role MVP:

2.1 The Contractor

The party responsible for specific work packages (e.g., blasting, transportation, excavation). The Contractor Portal is a mobile-friendly dashboard that tracks:

  • Digital License & Certificate Wallet: Uploads labour licenses, equipment fitness papers, and insurance. The system tracks expiry dates automatically.
  • Digital Attendance & Worker Roster: Replaces physical registers. Tracks daily headcount, PPE issuance, and worker training status.
  • Machinery Register: Tracks equipment status, ownership, and maintenance schedules.
  • Explosives Stock Register: Specifically for contractors with the blasting task type, tracking procured and used explosives securely.
  • Unresolved Action Center: A read-only feed showing active safety remarks or warnings from the Supervisor, demanding immediate attention.

2.2 The Supervisor

The party overseeing all contractors across the site. The Supervisor Portal absorbs traditional field inspector duties:

  • Contractor Status Dashboard: A bird's-eye view of all contractors, instantly surfacing invalid certifications and open penalties.
  • Observation Logger: A tool used during site walks to log safety violations (with photos and severity tags), instantly red-flagging the respective contractor.
  • Daily Yield & Environment Tracker: Logs coal tonnage and environmental readings (dust, effluent levels).
  • Platform-Wide Warning System: Deterministic rule-based flags trigger automatically when thresholds are breached.
  • Closure Verification: Supervisors review and approve proof of correction uploaded by contractors, permanently sealing the issue in an audit trail.

3. Dynamic Task Adaptation

MineSight uses a single task_type schema (blasting, transportation, excavation, maintenance) assigned during onboarding. Instead of separate portals, the interface conditionally renders modules based on this role. For example, a transportation contractor will not see the Explosives Stock Register, and the Machinery Register filters to show only relevant equipment.

4. AI Safety & Privacy

  • Zero Data Leakage: PII and credentials are never leaked. Passwords are securely hashed with bcrypt.
  • Deterministic AI: No hallucinated compliance. Warnings are driven solely by real data inputs and defined rules.
  • Strict RBAC: Contractors can only view their own data; Supervisors oversee the zone but cannot arbitrarily alter compliance definitions.
